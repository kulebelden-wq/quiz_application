QUIZARD APP - THE SIMPLE "HOW IT WORKS" GUIDE
(Explained like you're in primary school!)

Hello! This guide explains every little bit of code in your Quizard app. Think of the app like a toy house that we built together.

---

1. THE "BRAIN" OF THE APP (Java Files)
--------------------------------------

A. Question.java (The Question Box)
Imagine a small box. Inside, we put:
- The question (e.g., "What is 1+1?")
- Four cards with answers (e.g., "1", "2", "3", "4")
- A secret note saying which one is correct (e.g., "Answer #2 is right!")
- A label saying if it's "Math" or "Tech".
This file tells the computer how to make these boxes.

B. MainActivity.java (The Secret Door)
When you first touch the app icon, it opens this "Secret Door."
- It has one job: "Run as fast as you can to the Welcome Screen (LandingActivity)!"
- It doesn't stay open; it just pushes you through to the fun part.

C. LandingActivity.java (The Welcome Mat)
This is the big "Quizard" screen with your Brain Logo.
- It waits for you to click "Start playing."
- When you click it, it shows a "Selection Box" (Dialog).
- It asks: "Do you want Math or Tech?"
- Once you pick, it grabs your choice and says: "Go to the Quiz Room!"

D. QuizActivity.java (The Teacher)
This is the smartest part of the app.
- It holds all 20 questions in its memory.
- It checks: "Did the user pick Math?" If yes, it hides the Tech questions.
- It shows one question at a time.
- If you click a button, it turns Orange (to show you picked it).
- If you click "Check," it looks at its secret note.
- If you are right, it turns Green (Happy!). If wrong, it turns Red (Oops!).
- It adds +1 to your score every time you get it right.

E. ResultActivity.java (The Report Card)
After the 10th question, you come here.
- It takes your score and makes it a big percentage (like 100%!).
- It draws a big green circle around your score.
- It says "Passed Again!" to make you feel good.
- It has two buttons: "Go Back Home" or "Try Again."

---

2. THE "LOOKS" OF THE APP (XML Layout Files)
--------------------------------------------

A. activity_landing.xml (The Poster)
This is like drawing a poster.
- We painted the background Navy Blue.
- We put the "Quizard" title at the top in big white letters.
- We pasted your "Brain Logo" in the middle.
- We put the Cyan button at the bottom.

B. activity_quiz.xml (The Game Board)
This layout has:
- A purple bar at the top (The Header).
- A blue bar that grows (The Progress Bar).
- A floating dark card where the question sits.
- Four buttons for you to tap.

C. dialog_category_select.xml (The Popup)
This is the small window that pops up. It has your logo and two big buttons (Purple and Orange) so you can pick your favorite subject.

---

3. THE "CRAYON BOX" (Resources)
-------------------------------

A. colors.xml
This is our crayon box! Instead of saying "Blue," we give the computer a secret code (like #1A1B4B) so it always uses the exact same pretty Navy Blue.

B. drawable/ (The Shapes)
- quizard_btn_bg.xml: This tells the computer to cut the button corners so they are nice and round, not sharp.
- circle_blob.xml: This makes those soft circles in the background that look like bubbles.

---

4. THE "MAP" (AndroidManifest.xml)
This is the map for the whole phone. It tells the phone: "Hey! This app has a Welcome Screen, a Quiz Room, and a Result Page. Please let the user visit all of them!"

AND THAT'S IT! You built a real, professional app using these simple steps.
